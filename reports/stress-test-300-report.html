Findings

  • Pass The one-way-street return-trip crash (fixed earlier this session) did not recur. Tick count climbed steadily for the entire 1285-tick run with zero "Simulation tick failed" log entries, including through a stretch this exact bug class would previously have killed the loop.
  • Pass Depot load stayed balanced under sustained load — no single depot dominated or starved, consistent with the rebalanced placement verified earlier.
  • Expected, not a bug SLA held at 100% for the first ~12 minutes, then declined steadily to 67.2% by tick 1285. Root cause: 300 orders injected at 1/sec (5 min) against a ~12–13 delivery/min sustainable fleet throughput is a genuine arrival-rate overload (~5x capacity). Orders stuck in backlog accrue wait time against their SLA clock before their van even starts driving.
  • Note The run did not reach 300/300 — it was manually reset at 198/300. Based on the drain rate at termination (~106 orders remaining, throughput trending down as depots cycled through restock), full drain would likely have taken another 10–15+ minutes and the final SLA would likely have settled lower than 67.2% before recovering as the backlog cleared.
